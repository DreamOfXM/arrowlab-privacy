ArrowLab — Support

Quick answers

  • Where do I measure arrow length for FOC? From the throat of the nock to the end of the shaft, no point installed (AMO). Find the balance point on a fingertip.
  • Spine estimate disagrees with my chart? The bands are an estimator — your shaft maker's published chart wins.
  • Sight tape? Set two known marks, then verify the 30 and 60 marks on the range before trusting the tape.
  • Restore purchase? Settings → Unlock → Restore purchase.
